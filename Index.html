Pick Difficulty & Value

Season average odds per player · toughest pickers first · grade = how hard their picks were to hit
Implied % is the chance the odds give a pick of hitting (lower = harder). Avg multiplier is how much each of their legs multiplies a parlay payout on average. Wins vs expected compares actual wins to what the odds predicted (+ = beating the book). Difficulty grade: A = longest odds, F = safest. Odds tags: slip = from the group's bet slip; line = the sportsbook price posted before kickoff (ESPN/DraftKings moneylines, Covers closing spreads, bet365/FanDuel lines); alt = an alternate spread or total priced off that posted line (e.g. Lions -2.5 when the game closed -6.5); est = player props, estimated from typical prop lines.
